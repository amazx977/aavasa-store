Anti-Evaporation Formulation

Why Traditional Perfumes Fade in 1 Hour vs. Our 6+ Hours

The secret is in the distillation pyramid. Standard commercial perfumes front-load light citrus alcohol to impress you in the store, only to disappear within 60 minutes.

🌸 Top Notes (15–20% Ratio) 15–45 Minutes

Crushed Pink Pepper, Calabrian Bergamot, Lychee & Red Berries. The first vibrant burst that introduces the scent signature.

🌹 Heart Notes (40–45% Ratio) 2–4 Hours

Turkish Rose, Black Violet, Orange Blossom, Tuscan Leather & Saffron. The heart provides radiant floral sillage as your body warms.

🛡️ Heavy Base Anchors (35–40% Ratio) 6 to 9+ Hours

Madagascar Vanilla, Cambodian Oud, Ambroxan, Mysore Sandalwood & Ambergris. High molecular weight resinous fixatives that physically bond to skin proteins and resist heat degradation.

The 35–40% Base Advantage

In the Indian climate, high heat and humidity cause volatile fragrance molecules to evaporate at triple their normal speed. By increasing the heavy base fixatives to nearly double the industry standard, AAVASA creates an anti-evaporation shield.
